**Subject: The salon plan is real — and it's good. Need your brain on a few things.**

Hey Rory,

I've been putting together a real business plan for the salon idea, and I'm honestly kind of excited. A few things jumped out that I want you to hear first:

- **The location is wide open.** There's *no* salon-suite place anywhere in the Cadence ZIP codes — the nearest ones are 8–12 miles away. You'd be first, in one of the fastest-growing communities in the whole country.
- **You are the reason this works.** Sixteen years, the Great Clips management background, the fact that you *already* rent a suite so you know exactly what stylists want, and your client book coming with you — that's the stuff lenders actually care about. You're the asset.
- **One thing we have to be smart about:** there's a rule that can make a pure "we just rent out rooms" business ineligible for an SBA loan. It's fixable — we structure it as a membership/services business instead of a landlord — but I want us to confirm it with a free advisor before we lock in numbers. I'm handling that; just flagging it so it's not a surprise later.

The plan is basically done in draft form, but a big chunk of it is stuff only *you* can decide. Can you go through these and just reply right under each one? Rough answers are totally fine — "I don't know yet" is a real answer too.

**About you & your book**
1. Roughly how many regular clients do you have now, and what share of them do you think would follow you to Cadence?
2. About how much do you bring in a month right now from doing hair?
3. What do you pay for your current suite — and do you have a sense of what other suites/booths around Henderson go for?

**The name & the feel**
4. Any name ideas? (I've been calling the project "plume" as a placeholder.)
5. What's the vibe in your head — industrial, boho, luxe, gallery-white, desert-modern, something else? This one's all you.

**Pricing & what's included**
6. What would you want to charge — a monthly rate for the private rooms in back, and a daily (or part-time) rate for the chairs up front?
7. What's included in that price vs. what stylists pay for themselves? (utilities, wifi, towels/laundry, color/back-bar, booking software, parking…)
8. Do you want to sell retail products? And for the art on the walls — what cut would the salon take when a piece sells?
9. Do you want alcohol at events (wine at an art opening, etc.)? And would you ever "sponsor out" the gallery wall?

**Stylists & the space**
10. Do you already have specific stylists in mind who'd want in? Even two or three who'd commit early would make a huge difference.
11. Would you be open to taking over a space that *used to be a salon*? It's the single biggest way to keep costs down (the plumbing and sinks are already there).
12. For booking/scheduling — do you have a tool you like, or should I look into building something simple for us? (I've got options either way.)

**Money & timing**
13. Is there a number where it's just "too expensive to be worth it"? And would you stretch that for a plan you believed in?
14. Which strip mall on Lake Mead were you looking at? I couldn't remember which one.
15. When would you want to open — and is anything forcing the timeline (your current lease, a specific space)?
16. Last one, and it's a little personal so no pressure to put it in writing — roughly where's your credit at? Lenders look at it, so it just helps me know what we're working with.

No rush on all of it at once — even knocking out a few would help me keep moving. I've also got a rough pitch deck, a one-pager, and a "founding stylist" flyer drafted that we can polish once you've weighed in.

Proud of you for going for this.

Love,
Evan
